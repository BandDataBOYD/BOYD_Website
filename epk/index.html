Short bio

Formed in Raleigh, NC, in 2019, Bring Out Yer Dead is a premier Grateful Dead tribute band dedicated to authentically recreating the magic of the Dead's legendary live performances. Known for their meticulous attention to detail and dynamic musicianship, the band delivers immersive shows that span the Grateful Dead's extensive catalog—from their psychedelic '60s roots to their timeless jams of later decades. More than just a tribute band, Bring Out Yer Dead creates a communal celebration of music and memory, inviting fans old and new to relive the spirit of one of rock's most iconic bands.
