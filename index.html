Every era, every detail

Bring Out Yer Dead started in 2019 with one goal: recreate the feel of a real Grateful Dead show, not just the songs.

That means sweating the details — the instruments, the rigs, the stage layout, the two-drummer engine room — and pulling setlists from the whole catalog, from the psychedelic '60s through the '70s, '80s and beyond. Some nights it's cowboy songs and roadhouse blues; some nights the jam goes where it wants to go.

Whether you've been on the bus since the Fillmore days or you're catching your first "Scarlet › Fire," you're part of the show.
